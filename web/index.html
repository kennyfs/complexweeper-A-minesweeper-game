Complexweeper

How to play

The number on a cell is the modulus of the sum of the mines around it. Every mine is one of +1, −1, +i, −i (in the Minkowski mode +j, −j, with j² = +1 and the modulus √(a² − b²), so numbers can be imaginary). You win when the flags sit exactly on the mines and the flags around every open number add up to that number.

  • Mouse: left click opens, right click cycles the flag (none, +1, −1, +i/+j, −i/−j), middle click or Shift + click chords an open number.
  • Touch: use the Open / Flag switch; in Open mode tap a number to chord. A long press always flags.
  • Keys: → solver step, ← undo, Space auto play, F2 new game.
  • Solver: Step lets the solver make one move and explains it; Undo takes back any move.

All graphics are drawn in code (see tools/make_sprites.py) and released under GPL-3.0 with the rest of the project.
